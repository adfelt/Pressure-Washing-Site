# Off-Site SEO Checklist — Ranking for "Springfield Pressure Washing"

Goal: show up on page 1 and in the Google Map Pack (the 3 map results) for searches like
"springfield pressure washing", "pressure washing springfield or", and "pressure washers near me" in Springfield/Eugene.

The Map Pack is driven mostly by your **Google Business Profile (GBP)**, not the website.
Ranking factors, roughly: **relevance** (categories, services, content), **distance** (where the searcher is vs. your hidden address/service area), and **prominence** (reviews, citations, links).

## 🔴 Do First (this week)

### Google Business Profile
- [ ] **Primary category = "Pressure washing service".** This is the single biggest relevance signal. Double-check it.
- [ ] **Secondary categories** (add any that are true): Window cleaning service, Gutter cleaning service, Roof cleaning service (if available), Deck cleaning service (if available), Commercial cleaning service.
- [ ] **Service areas:** list Springfield first, then Eugene, Pleasant Hill, Coburg, Thurston, etc. Keep it under ~20 and within a ~1–2 hour drive (Google ignores huge areas).
- [ ] **Hidden address stays hidden** — you're set up correctly as a service-area business. Don't add your home address publicly.
- [ ] **Website link:** use `https://feltpressurewashing.com/` (consider UTM: `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp` so you can see GBP traffic in Analytics later).
- [ ] **Services section:** add every service (House Washing, Roof Cleaning, Driveway & Concrete, Deck & Fence, Window Cleaning, Commercial, Moss Prevention) with a 1–2 sentence description that mentions Springfield or Eugene naturally.
- [ ] **Business description (750 chars):** lead with "Felt Pressure Washing is a locally owned pressure washing company serving Springfield, Eugene, and Lane County, Oregon…"
- [ ] **Hours:** fill them in (even "Mon–Sat 8am–6pm"). Profiles open at search time get a boost. Then tell me the hours so I can add them to the site schema.

### Reviews (biggest prominence lever)
- [ ] Create a **direct review link** (GBP dashboard → "Ask for reviews") and save it on your phone.
- [ ] Ask **every** customer, same day, by text. Something like:
  > "Thanks again for having us out! If you were happy with the work, a quick Google review helps a ton — it'd be great if you mentioned what we cleaned and that you're in Springfield: [link]"
- [ ] Goal: **steady flow** (2–4/month) beats a burst. Reviews that mention "pressure washing" + "Springfield" help relevance.
- [ ] **Reply to every review** within a few days, naturally mentioning the service and city ("Glad we could get that moss off your roof in Thurston!").
- [ ] Never buy reviews or offer discounts in exchange for them — that gets profiles suspended.

## 🟠 Ongoing (weekly / monthly)

### GBP Activity
- [ ] **Upload photos after every job** (before + after). Take them on your phone at the job site. 2–5 per week is great.
- [ ] **Post a GBP Update weekly** — a before/after with 2–3 sentences: "Roof soft wash in Springfield's Gateway area — moss gone, shingles safe."
- [ ] Add a few **Q&A** entries yourself (e.g. "Do you serve Springfield?", "Is soft washing safe for my roof?") and answer them.
- [ ] Add **Products** (optional) — e.g. "House Wash — starting at $___" with a photo. Acts like extra listings inside your profile.

### Job-site signals (cheap & real)
- [ ] Yard sign at jobs ("Another clean home by Felt Pressure Washing — feltpressurewashing.com"), with neighbor permission.
- [ ] Door hangers to the 5–10 neighbors on each side after a job — brings in clustered local jobs and more Springfield reviews.
- [ ] Vehicle/trailer decal with the name, phone, and website.

## 🤔 The DBA Question — "Springfield Pressure Washing"

**Do the steps above first, then revisit in ~3 months.**

Keywords in the business name *are* a strong Map Pack ranking factor, and with a **real registered Oregon Assumed Business Name** it's legitimate (adding keywords to your GBP name *without* a legal name behind it violates Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended).

Downsides to weigh:
- Changing the GBP name triggers **re-verification** and invites spam reports from competitors — you'd need proof (signage, invoices, vehicle, website) that you genuinely operate under that name.
- You'd lose the **"Felt" brand** and existing name recognition; every citation above would need to be updated.
- "Springfield Pressure Washing" is **generic** — check the Oregon Secretary of State business registry to see if it's taken, and know you can't trademark/protect it.
- It ties you to Springfield and may **dilute Eugene**, which is the bigger market.

If you do go that route later: register the DBA first → update signage/invoices/truck → update the website → then update GBP and all citations at the same time.
